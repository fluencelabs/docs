# Claim tokens from the Fluence chain

Fluence closed its own chain, the Fluence rollup with chain ID 9999999, on 1 April 2026. Token balances from that chain moved to Ethereum mainnet. If you held FLT, pFLT, or USDC on the Fluence chain, claim them on Ethereum before 1 April 2027.

## What changed

- FLT and USDC balances on the Fluence chain moved to Ethereum mainnet.
- pFLT converted to FLT at a 1:1 ratio. The conversion includes staked pFLT and the staking rewards it had earned.
- Providers who earned FLT through the provider incentives program claim it with the same tool, without vesting.
- The Fluence bridge is disabled. Do not try to bridge funds from the Fluence chain.
- The chain's RPC endpoint, its block explorer, the bridge app, and the old staking app no longer work. Remove the Fluence network (chain ID 9999999) from your wallet.

## Claim your tokens

1. Open the [FLT Migration page](https://flt.fluence.ai/chain-migration) in the Fluence Token App.
2. Click **Connect Wallet** and connect the wallet whose address held the tokens on the Fluence chain.
3. Check the USDC and FLT amounts that the page shows for your address, and claim them.
4. Confirm the transaction in your wallet.

The Token App sends the tokens to the connected wallet on Ethereum mainnet. To earn rewards on the claimed FLT, [stake it](./overview.md).

Claims are open until 1 April 2027.
