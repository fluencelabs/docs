# Stake

> Guides for FLT holders. FLT is an ERC-20 token on Ethereum. Holders stake it in the Fluence Token App at flt.fluence.ai to earn FLT rewards and a share of protocol revenue.

Start with the [Overview](https://fluence.dev/docs/stake/overview.md). It explains how staking works and how to stake. The Fluence chain closed on 1 April 2026. If you held FLT, pFLT, or USDC on it, claim them on Ethereum before 1 April 2027, as described in [Claim tokens from the Fluence chain](https://fluence.dev/docs/stake/chain_migration.md).
